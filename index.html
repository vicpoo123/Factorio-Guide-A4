About Factorio

Learn about the challenge of building a factory and finding a way off an alien planet.

Factorio is an addictive game. You crash land on a deserted planet full of biters. You have to find a way to get off the planet without dying to the hostile natives.

The best way to get off the planet is to automate everything from the production of raw resources to transportation and finally to the refinement and crafting of these resources.

Be careful, the more you expand, the angrier the biters get, so make sure your weapons production is up to snuff.

There is always something to do in Factorio, a factory to create, or to improve.
